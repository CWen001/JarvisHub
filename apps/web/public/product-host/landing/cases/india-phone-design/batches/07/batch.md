# India Batch 07 — Paisley / Boteh Abstraction Control

## Schema question
Can explicit distance hierarchy and literal-copy exclusion preserve a readable Boteh relationship while keeping four proposals unmistakably mature 2026 smartphones?

## Fixed
- India-oriented mainstream or upper-mainstream slab
- about 80% Functional Form / 20% Cultural Expression
- complete bare-phone Dual-View Hero
- real two- or three-camera hierarchy with no filler
- one rigid material system and contrasting background
- no literal Paisley outline, repeat, engraving, or ornamental border

## Four visible strategies
1. **Flow enclosure** — full-to-tapered camera mass plus faint clear-coat attenuation
2. **Internal order** — mature rounded crossbar; Boteh carried inside lens-to-flash hierarchy and seam
3. **Surface routing** — compact vertical camera; Boteh carried by a broad-to-fine finish field away from hardware
4. **Edge cadence** — independent rings; Boteh carried by an asymmetrically narrowing functional rear chamfer

## Promotion rule
Retain only schema controls that improve more than one proposal: mature first read, one legible abstracted cultural relationship at normal size, hardware-first routing, and coherent view elevation. Do not promote one preferred colour or camera topology.
