# India Batch 13 — Mehndi Line Density as Process

## Schema question
Does a `departure_proof` block—target domain, revealing view, projected area and lighting task—make subtle craft processes visibly judgeable while Mehndi remains a bounded 20% layer?

## Fixed
- four 2026 India-oriented mainstream/upper-mainstream phones
- restrained-progressive; one clear Leading Departure and one named hero process
- Mehndi translated as controlled fine-line density, continuity and open/filled contrast
- exactly one bounded functional part carries linework
- no hands, floral vines, paisley repeats, bridal illustration, religious imagery or all-over ornament
- complete two-view bare phone, neutral screen and true hardware hierarchy

## Strategies
1. **Laser-etched camera saddle** — non-repeating contour-density field inside a rigid camera part
2. **Micro-embossed grip rail** — tactile fine-line cadence on one exposed side-rail zone
3. **IMT structural ribbon** — embedded nested-line rhythm inside one flush rear functional band
4. **Micro-milled lens court** — guilloché-like toolpath density around real lens retention

## Promotion rule
Keep departure proof only if the intended process is visibly inspectable in multiple outputs without resorting to exaggerated pattern scale or decorative close-up panels.
