# India Batch 09 — Premium Cultural Expression

## Design question

Can Schema 1.4.0 support stronger, high-aesthetic Indian cultural expression in a 2026 premium phone while preserving contemporary maturity, functional hierarchy, physical continuity and brand neutrality?

## Fixed

- 2026 India-oriented upper-mid/flagship slab
- approximately 65% Functional Form / 35% Cultural Expression, explicitly overriding the default 80/20 hierarchy
- exactly three real rear cameras and one subordinate flash
- one strong Leading Departure; remaining domains stay at the contemporary baseline
- one bounded premium cultural carrier plus one restrained supporting detail
- no literal monument, deity, sacred emblem, all-over ethnic print or copied competitor signature
- neutral front screen; coherent dual-view geometry
- background contrasts the phone in hue and luminance

## Four directions

1. **Bidri Midnight Inlay** — blackened-metal depth with silver-coloured inlay on a frame-fed camera shoulder
2. **Jali Shadow Glass** — warm ivory body with one under-glass solid–void camera court
3. **Stepwell Terrace** — deep peacock-teal body with a two-level camera-to-frame descent
4. **Meenakari Precision Edge** — midnight indigo body with controlled enamel-like colour cells on a functional camera retention bridge

## Promotion rule

Stronger cultural expression passes only when the cultural relationship is visible at normal hero size, belongs to a precise physical part, and strengthens rather than competes with camera function and whole-body form.
