# India Batch 08 — Mandala Radial Order

## Schema question
Can Mandala be translated into centre-to-periphery hierarchy and radial cadence that remains visibly legible at hero size without becoming a rosette graphic or symbolic circular phone?

## Fixed
- four independent 2026 India-oriented mainstream/upper-mainstream phones
- 80% Functional Form / 20% Cultural Expression
- exactly two consistent views per hero; neutral front screen
- conventional hardware-first camera topology, true lens/flash hierarchy
- one precise primary cultural carrier and one restrained supporting carrier
- no literal Mandala, flower, yantra, religious diagram, or decorative concentric print

## Four strategies
1. **Retention cadence** — radial hierarchy inside a compact camera court
2. **Crossbar focus** — centre-to-edge reflectivity rhythm within a horizontal enclosure
3. **Chamfer diffusion** — centre-to-periphery attenuation in functional edge facets
4. **Surface focus field** — a bounded radial satin process separated from independent camera hardware

## Promotion rule
Promote carrier legibility and limited cultural shape authority only if multiple outputs remain mature, physically specific, and culturally readable without illustrative graphics.
