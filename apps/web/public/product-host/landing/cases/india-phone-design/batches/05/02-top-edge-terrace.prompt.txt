Phone Design Kernel 0.4.0 learning batch. Create one brand-neutral 2026 India-oriented mid-range slab smartphone. The exterior thesis is a reliable large-screen phone whose horizontal camera mass grows coherently from the top frame rather than appearing pasted onto the back. Leading Departure: a top-edge camera terrace. Use about 80% Functional Form and 20% restrained architectural-shading expression.

Create exactly one landscape Phone Dual-View Hero containing exactly two consistent representations of the same bare phone and same colourway: rear three-quarter primary on the left and front secondary on the right. No panels, dividers, labels, lineup, extra device, hand, or person. Both views must agree on every product detail and use coherent perspective.

FORM SPINE: tall mainstream proportion; moderate continuous body and display corners; flat display, visually even narrow bezels, centred small punch hole; flat warm-silver central rail with softened contact edges; mostly flat back with one broad rear chamfer; credible medium volume and visible controls, antenna breaks, openings, and seams.

CAMERA ARCHITECTURE: form one shallow horizontal terrace immediately below the top edge, spanning about 72% of the back width but stopping clearly before both side rails. Its upper boundary visually continues the top-frame chamfer through a precise material seam; its lower boundary is a soft controlled step, not a floating black rectangle. Exactly three real lenses arranged horizontally: large main lens left, medium lens centre, smaller third lens right; a small flash sits below the third lens within the terrace. Rings have clear protection and descending scale. Use one elevation plus individual lens rings, no stacked plates. No full-width visor, fake lens, labels, status display, or light strip.

CULTURAL EXPRESSION AND CMF: low-saturation burnt-copper matte rear, warm silver frame, darker terracotta terrace, dark graphite lens rings, glossy optical glass. Add one very fine shaded groove along only the terrace lower edge, derived from architectural sun-shading rhythm; no facade pattern, arch, monument, or ornament. Keep the rest of the back calm.

Use a neutral charcoal low-information front screen. PRESENTATION: cool pale celadon and mist-grey backdrop, clearly cooler and lighter than the copper phone, with one broad soft diagonal shadow. Maintain strong hue and luminance contrast around the complete silhouette; do not use red, brown, orange, copper, burgundy, or tone-on-tone background. Premium realistic materials, clean grounding shadows, generous negative space.

No logo, brand name, case, bumper, doubled perimeter, rugged armour, fake aperture, decorative filler, watermark, copied competitor signature, malformed junction, impossible thinness, or unsupported claim.
