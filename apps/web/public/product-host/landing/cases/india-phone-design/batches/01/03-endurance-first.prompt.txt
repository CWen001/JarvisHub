Controlled smartphone design experiment, variant 3 of 4: endurance-first visible value. Design a brand-neutral premium 2026 6.7-inch mainstream slab smartphone positioned at INR 45,000–60,000 for India. Keep every shared attribute identical to the fixed brief; change only how functional value is prioritized. The first impression must communicate believable long-use endurance, durability, and secure grip through proportion and junction quality, without rugged styling or unsupported performance claims.

Create exactly one continuous Phone Dual-View Hero with exactly two consistent representations of the same bare phone and same deep mineral-green colourway: a large rear three-quarter primary view on the left and a smaller front secondary view on the right. No panels, labels, captions, lineup, extra phone, hand, or person. Both views must match in silhouette, corner family, apparent thickness, camera count, controls, seams, colour, and finish. Each view uses one physically coherent projection and occlusion.

Use the same tall 6.7-inch-class proportion and softly squared medium-radius corners, but express honest internal volume with a slightly fuller, still-premium side profile rather than false wafer thinness. Use flat front glass, visually even narrow bezels, centred small punch hole, satin gunmetal frame with softened contact edges, and a broad controlled rear-to-frame transition that looks secure in the hand. The front screen is neutral low-information charcoal grey with restrained monochrome time and status elements; it does not echo rear geometry.

Use deep mineral-green finely matte glass on the rear. Keep the three-camera architecture compact and low: exactly three real lenses in a clear two-plus-one hierarchy on a shallow upper-left terrace whose outer boundary flows into the side frame. Use a wide load-bearing-looking base, protected lens rims, clean sealed-looking seams, believable elevation, and no armor graphics. The camera is supporting rather than dominant. Express endurance only through honest body volume, comfortable rails, stable camera stance, precise junctions, and restrained anti-fingerprint-looking matte finish; do not show battery icons, shields, bolts, rubber bumpers, or claim capacity, IP, drop, thermal, or structural validation.

Resolve separate tactile volume and faceted power controls on the right side, subtle antenna breaks, clean assembly seams, and plausible USB-C, speaker, microphone, and SIM details only where the angle permits. The chassis is one naked phone, never a case. CMF remains fixed: matte mineral green, satin gunmetal, glossy optical glass, no accent colour.

Use the same neutral warm-grey seamless studio background, soft premium retail lighting, realistic contact shadows, controlled highlights, and generous negative space. Preserve complete inspectable bodies.
