# India Batch 12 — Elephant: Calm Mass and Protection

## Schema question
Can a compact positive process specification—one process, one zone, one visual effect, one boundary—produce bolder but mature designs while translating elephant-associated stability and protection without animal or devotional imagery?

## Fixed
- four 2026 India-oriented mainstream/upper-mainstream phones
- 80% Functional Form / 20% Cultural Expression
- restrained-progressive, one clear Leading Departure
- Elephant qualities only: calm mass, grounded stance, protective overhang, load-bearing continuity
- no Ganesha depiction, elephant face, trunk, ears, tusks, animal silhouette or temple ornament
- complete bare Dual-View Hero, true camera hierarchy, continuous chassis

## Strategies
1. **Machined protective brow** — CNC-machined anodized aluminium above a compact camera court
2. **Forged camera saddle** — bead-blasted forged aluminium carrier connecting camera mass to frame
3. **Grounded grip rail** — laser-structured microtexture on one broad lower side-rail grip zone
4. **Ceramic shoulder** — UV-cured ceramic-effect hardcoat on a rigid colour-matched camera shoulder

## Promotion rule
Keep the focused process rule if multiple images show a specific physical effect without decorative drift. Judge cultural quality through structural character, not icon recognizability.
