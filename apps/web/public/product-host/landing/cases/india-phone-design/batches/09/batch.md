# India Batch 09 — Lotus Layered Emergence

## Schema question
Can explicit flush-surface and continuous-perimeter constraints translate Lotus through nested opening, buoyant layering, and clean emergence without literal petals or case-like parts?

## Fixed
- four independent 2026 India-oriented mainstream/upper-mainstream phones
- 80% Functional Form / 20% Cultural Expression
- exactly two consistent views, neutral front, credible complete bare chassis
- hardware-first camera architecture; no fake apertures
- cultural carriers remain flush/borderless or correspond to one precise rigid part
- no lotus illustration, petal outline, floral engraving, religious icon, or decorative bloom

## Strategies
1. **Nested lens seats** — opening curvature inside functional camera retention
2. **Layered crossbar** — shallow rigid camera layers with centre-to-edge lift
3. **Finish emergence** — borderless matte-to-satin attenuation across one continuous rear
4. **Chamfer opening** — continuous rear-to-frame transition broadens and resolves without corner caps

## Promotion rule
Promote body-continuity controls only if they prevent panel/case readings across multiple outputs. Treat Lotus readability separately from generic elegance.
