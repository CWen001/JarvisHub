Create a brand-neutral 2026 India-oriented upper-mainstream smartphone titled “Quiet Boteh Flow”. Exterior thesis: a mature soft-square imaging phone whose camera mass and rear finish share one calm full-to-tapered directional flow. Leading Departure: a shallow satin camera enclosure begins as a broad rounded main-camera anchor and narrows into a precise tail, echoed only by a low-contrast clear-coat light field on the rear. About 80% Functional Form and 20% restrained Cultural Expression. At retail distance it must read only as a credible current smartphone; at hand distance the Boteh relationship becomes perceptible; no literal Paisley outline, curl, engraving, repeated motif, textile print, or ornamental border.

Create exactly one landscape Phone Dual-View Hero containing exactly two consistent representations of this same bare phone and same warm mineral-silver colourway: rear three-quarter primary on the left and front secondary on the right. No panels, labels, lineup, extra device, hand, or person. Both views agree in silhouette, corners, frame, thickness, controls, camera hardware, seams, colour, and finish. Coherent projection and physically credible occlusion.

FORM SPINE: tall 6.7-inch-class proportion, believable medium thickness, moderate continuous soft-square radii shared by body and display, flat central satin frame with softened micro-chamfers, nearly flat front glass, one controlled rear edge transition, secure rather than razor-thin grip. Modern visually even narrow bezels, centred small punch hole, neutral low-information charcoal screen unrelated to the exterior.

CAMERA ARCHITECTURE: upper-left shallow horizontal enclosure with clear top and side margins. Exactly three real lenses and exactly one flash. One largest protected main lens in the broad rounded anchor; two smaller true lenses descend clearly in scale along one horizontal axis toward the right; one much smaller pill flash terminates the tapered tail. No other dot, hole, sensor, text, light, aperture, or filler. One shallow enclosure step, protected rings, one precise hard-part seam and narrow attachment shadow. The taper must remain visibly asymmetric and directional, not become a generic capsule or full-width visor.

REAR AND CMF: warm mineral-silver fine matte rigid rear, slightly darker champagne-silver satin frame and enclosure, dark glossy optical glass. One transparent clear-coat field begins broad below the main-camera anchor and attenuates diagonally toward the lower-right as a soft change in reflectivity, not a drawn line. It routes around all hardware and fades before the perimeter. Reflectivity hierarchy: optics high, rings/enclosure medium, broad rear low. No more than three physically legible finish zones.

CONTROLS AND ASSEMBLY: coherent right-side power and volume keys, restrained antenna breaks, charging opening, speaker and microphone perforations, SIM seam and assembly boundaries only where the view permits. No case-like doubled perimeter.

FAMILY GENES: soft-square body/display radius agreement; asymmetric full-to-tapered camera mass; one broad-to-fine finish flow. CULTURAL SOURCE: Paisley/Boteh abstracted only as fullness transitioning into directional taper and attenuation. Primary carrier is the camera enclosure outer mass; supporting carrier is the subtle clear-coat field.

PRESENTATION: premium but attainable product photography, 70–80% frame occupancy, deep muted indigo background clearly darker and cooler than the phone, faint warm sandstone haze, soft large-source lighting, precise rim separation, grounded shadows, no literal Indian prop or architecture.

Forbidden: real logo or brand, copied competitor signature, literal Paisley motif, engraving, ornament, all-over pattern, four cameras, fake optics, generic black rectangle, full-width camera bar, case, bumper, rugged armour, floating part, melted seam, impossible thinness, pseudo text, watermark, hand, person, unsupported performance or manufacturing claim.
