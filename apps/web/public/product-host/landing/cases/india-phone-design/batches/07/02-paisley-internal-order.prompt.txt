CAMERA ARCHITECTURE: one upper horizontal rigid crossbar with clear margins and one shallow step. Exactly three real lenses and one flash, all on one horizontal centreline: largest protected main lens on left; two true auxiliary lenses decreasing in diameter; one much smaller pill flash at far right. No literal Paisley, repeated pattern, engraving, floral line, or textile print. Reflectivity: optics high, rings/enclosure medium, rear low.
