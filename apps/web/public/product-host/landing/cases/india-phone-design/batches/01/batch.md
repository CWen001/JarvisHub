# India Batch 01 — Visible Value Hierarchy

## Hypothesis

For a ₹45,000–₹60,000 India-market flagship, a deliberate visible hierarchy between imaging, endurance/durability, and retail recognition will produce a more credible first-read value proposition than a generic global-premium exterior.

## Fixed brief

- Brand-neutral 2026 6.7-inch mainstream slab smartphone
- Deep mineral green matte glass, satin gunmetal frame, three real cameras
- Flat display, visually even narrow bezels, centered punch hole
- Exactly one Phone Dual-View Hero with rear three-quarter primary and front secondary
- Same studio composition, projection discipline, lighting, and exclusions

## Compared axis

How functional value is prioritized and made visible through proportion, Camera Architecture, frame/back transition, part boundaries, and grip character.

1. Control — current global-premium baseline
2. Camera-first — imaging is primary
3. Endurance-first — battery, durability, and grip are primary
4. Balanced — imaging primary; endurance/durability supporting; retail recognition tertiary

## Evaluation

- Is the first-read value proposition clear at 1–2 metres?
- Does the phone remain mature and premium rather than decorative or ruggedized?
- Is each implied function supported by visible physical architecture?
- Does the design remain credible under close inspection and likely case use?
