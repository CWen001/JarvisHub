# Phone Design Judgment Schema 0.2.0

Use this Schema as a linked generation chain for smartphone exterior design. It defaults toward India-market priorities while preserving the 2026 Contemporary Baseline. It is not a questionnaire, product brief, market strategy, or performance specification.

## Industrial-design remit

Treat product class, dimensions, hardware, battery, camera specification, durability targets, cost constraints, and launch role as supplied inputs. Ask only when a missing fact would materially change exterior form. Use familiar classes—entry, mainstream, upper-mid, flagship—rather than making the designer reason from local currency.

Resolve silhouette, proportion, visual thickness, corner and chamfer family, grip transitions, front boundary, frame, controls and openings, rear surface, Camera Architecture, CMF, and assembly expression. Never invent product requirements to justify a shape.

## Generation chain

1. **Brief boundary** — Separate supplied product decisions from exterior decisions still open. Preserve fixed hardware and requirements; do not silently redefine the product.
2. **India-oriented reading** — Select only the India-market conditions that bear on this exterior: handheld comfort under substantial body volume, clear value at retail distance, close-view material credibility, likely case use, expressive or quiet CMF, and culturally appropriate character. Do not force every condition into every phone.
3. **Exterior thesis** — State one sentence linking the intended first impression to one whole-body relationship. The thesis must name form, not merely a user segment, feature, mood, or cultural theme.
4. **Form spine** — Derive one continuous relationship through body aspect, corner radius, front-glass boundary, chamfer, frame profile, rear transition, visual thickness, and grip. A change at one point must propagate to adjacent surfaces rather than becoming isolated styling.
5. **Camera Architecture** — Resolve the supplied camera hardware through count, scale hierarchy, arrangement, elevation, protection, part boundaries, shadow gaps, and attachment to the rear or frame. Decide whether it is primary, supporting, or quiet; never add fake optics to increase perceived value.
6. **Exterior consequences** — Translate supplied functional requirements only into credible form consequences. Battery volume may affect thickness and balance; durability may affect glass exposure, corner treatment, lens protection, seams, and grip; imaging hardware may affect camera mass and hand clearance. Suggest accommodation without claiming validated capacity, strength, ingress, thermal, radio, or optical performance.
7. **India character** — When it improves the concept, carry local character through one coherent relationship in form, material division, colour behaviour, texture, finish, or naming. Keep it subordinate to product integrity. Avoid mandatory motifs, stereotyped palettes, and cultural decoration pasted onto a generic phone.
8. **Material and craft hierarchy** — Assign a small number of physically legible zones, one reflectivity hierarchy, appropriate texture scale, controlled accents, and credible junction quality for the product class. Premium expression comes from relationship and finish discipline, not automatically from more gloss, gold, rings, or parts.
9. **Family genes** — Define two or three repeatable geometric, camera-integration, control, or CMF relationships when family continuity matters. For a requested series, establish the shared form spine before varying each member; otherwise do not manufacture a lineup.
10. **Uncontaminated dual-view proof** — Show one bare phone consistently across front and rear. Keep the screen neutral and independent so wallpaper, presentation graphics, or case-like perimeter features cannot impersonate exterior design.

## India-oriented judgment floor

Use market evidence directionally:

- India contains several product classes and aesthetic modes; do not encode one national taste.
- Large displays and substantial batteries make honest volume, visual thinness, chamfers, grip, and camera balance recurring exterior questions.
- Offline handling and likely case use increase the value of silhouette, camera outer contour, side-frame and control identity, and close-view junction quality; do not assume an uncovered back graphic will carry recognition.
- Camera, endurance, durability, display, and performance may be important product messages, but the exterior should express only consequences supported by supplied hardware or requirements.
- Expressive colour and effect CMF are available tools, not defaults. Quiet global-premium design can also fit India.
- Local character is stronger when it changes a real material or form relationship than when it appears as a literal symbol.

## Synthesis

A ready concept has one exterior thesis, one Leading Departure, a continuous Form Spine, resolved Camera Architecture, three to five visible decisions, explicit preservation and exclusion constraints, and no unresolved consequential conflict. If several messages compete for first read, subordinate all but one.

## Learning

User feedback may be as simple as liking or disliking images or identifying a problem. Route it proportionally:

- one-off colour, theme, or request preference → current concept;
- recurring weakness in questions or indistinct proposed directions → Design Dialogue;
- reusable relationship among form, grip, camera, exterior consequence, India character, or CMF → this Schema;
- contemporary identity, physical continuity, projection, or artifact validity failure → Base Contract.

Promote only what the feedback supports. Do not derive a universal preference from one isolated image, and do not let generation or delivery diagnostics become design rules.
