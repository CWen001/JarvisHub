# India Batch 03 — Functional Form 60 / Cultural Expression 40

## Prototype question

Can one mature India-oriented mid-range phone brief produce four visibly different, culturally specific exterior concepts without losing functional credibility or falling into motif pastiche?

## Fixed Functional Form

- 2026 mid-range mainstream slab smartphone
- moderate body and display corner radii
- believable medium body volume, flat active display, narrow flat central rail with controlled chamfers
- three real cameras in a 2+1 hierarchy, one flash
- comfortable-looking grip and coherent assembly
- exactly one Dual-View Hero per concept

## Cultural translations

1. Indian textile craft — weave hierarchy and border density
2. Stone and inlay craft — mineral field and restrained inlay
3. Modern Indian architecture — shading depth and stepped structural rhythm
4. Monsoon regional nature — wet/dry reflectivity and layered atmospheric light

Each concept uses one primary carrier on the phone, one supporting carrier, and a matching restrained presentation atmosphere. Cultural Expression is visible but subordinate to the complete smartphone.
