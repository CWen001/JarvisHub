# India Batch 04 — Restrained Cultural Expression 80/20

## Prototype question

Does reducing Cultural Expression from 40% to 20% preserve mature mid-range phone form while retaining readable India character?

## Fixed Functional Form

- 2026 India-oriented mid-range slab smartphone
- moderate body and display corner radii
- believable medium volume
- flat active display, narrow flat graphite rail, small front chamfer, broader softened rear chamfer
- identical compact upper-left three-camera 2+1 architecture and flash
- camera position and optical hierarchy resolve independently from cultural fields
- landscape Dual-View Hero with restrained culture-bearing atmosphere

## Cultural variants

1. Textile craft: indigo micro-weave field offset from the camera
2. Stone and inlay: sandstone mineral finish with one restrained green inlay line
3. Modern architecture: terracotta material with one shaded edge rhythm
4. Monsoon nature: storm-teal wet/dry finish transition
