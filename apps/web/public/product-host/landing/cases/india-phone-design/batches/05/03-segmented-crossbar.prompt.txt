Phone Design Kernel 0.4.0 learning batch. Create one brand-neutral 2026 India-oriented mid-range slab smartphone. The exterior thesis is a light, modern phone with a horizontal camera crossbar whose functional hierarchy is visible through two restrained material segments, not a generic black slab. Leading Departure: a segmented crossbar. Use about 80% Functional Form and 20% restrained mineral-inlay expression.

Create exactly one landscape Phone Dual-View Hero containing exactly two consistent representations of the same bare phone and same colourway: rear three-quarter primary on the left and front secondary on the right. No panels, labels, lineup, extra device, hand, or person. Both views must match exactly and obey coherent perspective.

FORM SPINE: tall mainstream display proportion; softly squared moderate corners; flat active display with visually even narrow bezels and centred small punch hole; narrow flat pale-silver rail with softened edge breaks; mostly flat back and broad gentle rear transition; believable medium thickness, coherent controls, openings, antenna breaks, and seams.

CAMERA ARCHITECTURE: position a horizontal rounded crossbar in the upper back with equal top and side breathing room, spanning about 78% of the width. Divide it once at a precise vertical seam: a short dark left segment carries one large protected main lens; a longer pale right segment carries exactly two smaller real lenses aligned horizontally plus one tiny flash. The two segments share one outer silhouette and one shallow elevation, while the seam clarifies hierarchy. Lens size descends clearly. The crossbar ends use the same corner logic as the body and blend into the back through a narrow shadow gap. No full-width edge-to-edge visor, fake hole, fourth lens, text, screen, or light.

CULTURAL EXPRESSION AND CMF: pale mineral-jade matte rear; satin silver frame; charcoal main-camera segment; slightly darker jade auxiliary segment; glossy optics. A hairline warm-grey inlay marks only the segment seam and repeats once on the power key. No stone patchwork, mosaic, jewellery, floral motif, or literal craft object. Keep the lower back uninterrupted.

Use a neutral dark-grey low-information front screen. PRESENTATION: deep muted aubergine and charcoal-violet studio field, clearly darker and complementary to the pale jade phone, with soft warm rim light and restrained haze. Maintain clean silhouette separation; do not use green, jade, mint, silver, white, or tone-on-tone background. Realistic product rendering and grounding shadows with generous negative space.
