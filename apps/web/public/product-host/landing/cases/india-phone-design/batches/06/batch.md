# India Batch 06 — Paisley Series-System Candidate

## Schema question

Can one nested series-level Schema produce four visibly related members while keeping camera hierarchy, rigid-material junctions, and Paisley/Boteh cultural expression coherent?

## Small task brief

Design a 2026 India-oriented mainstream-to-upper-mid phone family informed by Transsion's retail-distance camera recognition and youthful CMF. All four members share a soft-square slab and an upper rigid horizontal camera crossbar: a full rounded left anchor flows into a gently tapered right tail. Every real lens and the single subordinate tail flash share one horizontal axis. Paisley is translated as a structural relationship, never pasted as an all-over print.

## Members

1. Base — graphite black, two cameras, shortest crossbar, contour-only Paisley
2. Standard — peacock teal, three cameras, directional microtexture
3. Pro — moonstone silver, enlarged main camera, transparent-layer light flow
4. India Edition — restrained pomegranate red, Standard hardware, one non-repeating micro-engraved Paisley field

## Fixed presentation

Each member receives one consistent Phone Dual-View Hero. Framing and light softness remain comparable; each background contrasts its phone in hue and luminance.

## Promotion rule

The candidate Schema is not authoritative. Promote it only if the rendered family reads as one series and the user confirms that camera/flash order, rigid-part junctions, and Paisley integration improve consistently.
