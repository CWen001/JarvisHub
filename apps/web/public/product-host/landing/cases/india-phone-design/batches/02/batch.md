# India Batch 02 — Chamfer, frame, rear transition, and grip

## Fixed

- India-oriented mid-range 2026 slab smartphone
- Moderate body and display corner radii
- Believable medium thickness
- Flat active display, visually even narrow bezels, centered punch hole
- Identical compact upper-left three-camera 2+1 architecture
- Matte deep blue-grey rear, satin graphite frame, no accent colour
- Identical dual-view composition and lighting

## Four implementations

1. Narrow flat rail with small front and rear planar chamfers
2. Flat front with a broad rear curve flowing into the rail
3. Symmetric subtle 2.5D front and rear edge arcs around a narrow central rail
4. Broader flat rail with localized precision facets

## Question

Which body transition produces the best combination of mature mid-range identity, comfortable-looking grip, believable volume, and India-market relevance?
