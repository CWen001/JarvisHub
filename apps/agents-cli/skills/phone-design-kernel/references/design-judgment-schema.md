# Phone Design Judgment Schema 1.4.0

Use [`phone-design-judgment.schema.json`](phone-design-judgment.schema.json) as the authoritative nested model for India-oriented smartphone exterior judgment. Resolve it semantically; do not expose it as a questionnaire or paste its field names into the provider prompt.

## Scope

Product class, dimensions, hardware, battery, camera specification, durability targets, cost constraints, and launch role are supplied inputs. Ask only when a missing fact materially changes exterior form. Use entry, mainstream, upper-mid, and flagship rather than requiring designers to reason from local currency.

The Schema owns silhouette, proportion, visual thickness, corner and chamfer family, grip transitions, front boundary, frame, controls and openings, rear surface, Camera Architecture, CMF, assembly expression, Cultural Expression, and presentation atmosphere. It does not make product-strategy or hardware decisions.

## Nested generation order

1. **design_brief** — separate fixed product requirements from open exterior decisions.
2. **functional_form** — resolve about 80% of design attention through silhouette and volume; corner, chamfer and grip; front, frame and controls; Camera Architecture; rear surface; and Exterior Consequences of supplied requirements.
3. **cultural_expression** — resolve about 20% through one source family, one abstracted relationship, one primary surface or material carrier, and one restrained supporting detail.
4. **concept_identity** — synthesize one exterior thesis, one Leading Departure, three to five visible decisions, and Family Genes when relevant.
5. **presentation** — prove the design with a consistent Phone Dual-View Hero, a culture-bearing but subordinate atmosphere, and visible proof of the Leading Departure.

The 80/20 split is an attention hierarchy, not a measured area or prompt-length quota. A brief may override it for an art edition, festival edition, or intentionally neutral product.

## Controlled originality

Set one explicit innovation budget relative to the 2026 Contemporary Baseline. Name the departure domain, preserve the rest of the baseline, and require a normal-size difference test so restraint does not collapse into sameness.

Judge Camera Architecture originality as one combination of enclosure outline, lens-node arrangement, flash position, frame relationship, and CMF. Common primitive shapes remain available, but their combination must not reproduce a recognizable released-product signature. Resolve perimeter continuity and rear body continuity at the same time so originality does not become a pasted-on island.

For every named appearance process, bind one process to its substrate, physical zone, visible signature, feature scale, boundary behaviour, reflectivity and colour behaviour, and plausibility limit. Finish changes stay flush and borderless unless they correspond to one intentional rigid assembled part.

## Cultural authority

Functional Form resolves the phone’s major body volume, camera position, optical hierarchy, and primary camera-island topology before Cultural Expression. Cultural Expression may enrich rear surfaces, material processes, pattern fields, colour behaviour, finish fields, local edge details, fine boundaries, micro-texture, controls, or restrained accents.

A cultural field terminates, routes around, or supports camera hardware. It does not place lenses ambiguously on a decorative boundary. It may alter primary massing or camera topology only when the brief explicitly makes cultural form the product’s Leading Departure.

Use one source family per concept—material craft, architectural relationship, regional nature, festival atmosphere, or a source supplied by the brief. Extract a relationship rather than copying a symbol. Quiet execution still requires a readable source-to-relationship-to-carrier chain.

State the observed source, extracted relationship, literal-copy exclusion, viewing-distance hierarchy, carrier legibility, and limits on shape authority. Confine expression to one named physical carrier with an explicit boundary and spillover exclusion. Sacred or devotional sources contribute only non-iconic spatial, material, or structural relationships unless the brief explicitly authorizes religious imagery. Exact-count cadence belongs only at process scale on a real functional part; it must not become an emblem or oversized pseudo-hardware.

## India-oriented floor

- India contains several product classes and aesthetic modes; never encode one national taste.
- Large displays and substantial batteries make honest volume, visual thinness, chamfers, grip, and camera balance recurring exterior questions.
- Offline handling and likely case use increase the value of silhouette, camera outer contour, side-frame identity, controls, and close-view junction quality.
- Express only exterior consequences supported by supplied hardware or requirements; never claim validated performance from an image.
- Expressive colour, craft and pattern are legitimate when subordinate to mature phone form and physically tied to surfaces or part boundaries.
- Presentation should extend the selected cultural source through restrained colour, haze, material light, or abstract shadow rather than returning every design to a neutral-grey studio.

## Synthesis

A ready design resolves every required nested group, one exterior thesis, one Leading Departure, a continuous Form Spine, coherent Camera Architecture, credible Exterior Consequences, a restrained Cultural Expression, and a subordinate Presentation Atmosphere. If several messages compete for first read, Functional Form wins.

## Learning

User feedback may simply identify liked or disliked images, something to preserve, or a visible problem. Apply it proportionally:

- one-off colour or theme preference → current concept;
- weak questions or indistinct directions → Design Dialogue;
- a reusable form, camera, cultural-carrier, CMF, or trade-off relationship → nested Schema;
- contemporary identity or physical-validity failure → Base Contract.

Do not infer a universal rule from one isolated image. Delivery diagnostics do not become design judgments.
