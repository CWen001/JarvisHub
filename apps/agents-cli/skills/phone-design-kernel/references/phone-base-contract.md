# Phone Base Contract 0.1.3

Every Phone Exterior Concept must satisfy this visible-validity contract before aesthetic preference is judged.

## 1. Contemporary phone identity

The first read is a complete, production-plausible smartphone current to the 2026 baseline. Preserve the accepted product position, Market Context, form factor, and requested changes. Avoid legacy restyling and speculative post-phone objects unless explicitly requested.

## 2. Dual-View Hero invariant

Default to one continuous premium marketing frame containing exactly two consistent representations of the same phone design and colourway:

- one rear or rear-three-quarter primary view;
- one supporting front view;
- no panels, dividers, labels, contact sheet, technical orthographic board, exploded view, lineup, spare device, hand, or person;
- both phones remain complete enough to inspect, without identity-bearing edges cropped.

The two views must agree on silhouette, corner family, frame profile, apparent thickness, camera count and placement, control positions, seams, materials, colour, and finish. A mismatch rejects the image.

Each representation must obey one coherent camera projection. Surface visibility, foreshortening, parallel-edge convergence, and occlusion must agree with its viewpoint: a front view seen from level or above cannot simultaneously expose the phone's underside. Reject a compound viewpoint, locally rotated face, or port-bearing edge revealed from the wrong elevation.

## 3. Naked whole-body continuity

The image shows an unaccessorized bare phone. Resolve it as connected physical zones: display/glass, front boundary, one chassis frame, rear surface, camera enclosure and hardware, controls, openings, seams, and transitions. Curves and part boundaries meet cleanly without melted corners, overlaps, floating components, doubled edges, or malformed junctions.

The chassis must read unmistakably as the phone itself. Reject a doubled perimeter, bumper silhouette, raised protective lip, wraparound outer shell, case-like corner buildup, or inset back with clearance that makes the product appear to wear a removable case. Any rail or perimeter feature must prove it is integral through continuous controls, antenna breaks, openings, and assembly seams.

Visual thinness must come from coherent edge, frame, back, and camera transitions rather than impossible wafer geometry or hidden hardware.

## 4. Evaluation-neutral front

Require:

- a modern display proportion with deliberate, visually even edge treatment;
- mutually coherent body and display corner radii;
- one intentional front-camera treatment;
- a neutral, low-information screen background used only to reveal bezel, glass, and silhouette;
- no reuse of the rear design's signature geometry, accent colour, texture, pattern, or Family Gene on the screen unless the user explicitly requests interface design;
- no unexplained thick chin, random asymmetric bezel, pseudo-text, app-grid clutter, glowing sci-fi HUD, or wallpaper that competes with exterior evaluation.

## 5. Camera Architecture

Show only deliberate functional elements. Lenses, flash, auxiliary sensors, enclosure, rear surface, and frame form one hierarchy. Their scale, count, alignment, protrusion, material boundaries, and shadows remain consistent across visible views.

An identity-bearing camera integration must visibly prove its claimed relationship through a controlled part boundary, retention cue, surface transition, or frame connection. Naming a spine, bridge, court, plateau, or recess does not qualify when the pixels resolve as a generic island, decorative accent, or melted depression.

Reject fake lens fillers, arbitrary rings, repeated decorative apertures, unsupported optics labels, a generic black rectangle pasted onto the back, or camera mass disconnected from grip and whole-body balance.

## 6. Controls, openings, and assembly

Expected controls and openings are visible where the chosen angle permits or are explicitly resolved in the prompt: power and volume controls, speaker/microphone openings, charging opening, SIM treatment when applicable, antenna breaks when visible, and assembly seams. Use coherent scale, spacing, alignment, and boundary treatment.

## 7. CMF integrity

Assign material, colour, finish, texture scale, reflectivity, and light response by physical zone. Keep a clear reflectivity hierarchy and controlled accent budget. Finish changes follow real part boundaries or intentional surface fields rather than random decoration.

## 8. Brand neutrality and family readiness

Use no real logo, brand name, copied competitor signature, fake specification, watermark, or released-product claim. Preserve a credible identity zone and two or three repeatable Family Genes without automatically showing a product family.

## 9. Visible credibility only

The image may demonstrate coherent visible architecture. It must not claim validated optics, strength, thermal behavior, radio performance, battery capacity, ingress protection, manufacturing feasibility, sustainability, or sales suitability.

## Gate

Any failure above returns Reject. Preserve and deliver the image with the first failed rule and visible evidence; never hide or automatically replace it.
