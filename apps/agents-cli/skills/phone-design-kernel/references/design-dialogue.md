# Phone Design Dialogue 1.4.0

Use dialogue to improve exterior design decisions, not to redefine the product or collect every field. The default market reading is India-oriented. Ask only questions whose answers materially change visible form.

## Turn 1 — Brief and exterior thesis

Read supplied product decisions as fixed inputs. Infer obvious facts. When shape-critical information is genuinely missing, ask one compact question covering only the unresolved items, such as product class, body-size or thickness intent, display boundary, supplied camera hardware, and durability or battery-volume constraint. Use entry, mainstream, upper-mid, and flagship rather than local-currency bands unless the user supplied a price.

When the user has not supplied a cultural direction, ask at most one further design question: which source family should carry India character—material craft, architectural relationship, regional nature, or festival atmosphere—and should its expression be restrained, balanced, or vivid? If the user delegates the choice, use the restrained 80% Functional Form / 20% Cultural Expression default and recommend visibly different source translations.

Then state:

- a concise professional reading of the exterior-design task;
- the one consequential tension among proportion, grip, thickness, Camera Architecture, CMF, and India character;
- normally three visibly different geometric strategies, or the user-requested count, with the first recommended.

Describe each strategy through visible relationships—corner and chamfer family, frame/back transition, camera attachment, rear division, thickness character—not adjective variants or abstract feature priorities. Give one honest trade-off.

## Turn 2 — Visible relationship

Build on the accepted thesis and resolve the Form Spine, Camera Architecture, and exterior consequences of supplied requirements. Then resolve Cultural Expression as a source family, one extracted design relationship, one primary surface or material carrier, one restrained supporting detail, and their connection to Functional Form. Camera position and optical hierarchy resolve first; cultural fields terminate, route around, or support them rather than placing lenses ambiguously on a decorative boundary. Resolve a Presentation Atmosphere from the same source without asking the background to manufacture identity the phone lacks. Offer alternatives only where one consequential exterior relationship remains open. Do not reopen settled product definition.

Dialogue is complete when there is one accepted thesis, one Leading Departure, three to five visible decisions, a source-to-relationship-to-carrier Cultural Expression, a subordinate Presentation Atmosphere, preservation and exclusion constraints, and no unresolved exterior conflict.

## Turn 3 — Conflict only

Ask only when the reply creates a real conflict, such as demanding both minimal camera presence and unmistakable long-range camera identity. Otherwise use professional defaults and proceed.

## Learning Batch

One accepted dialogue covers the whole requested batch. A batch may be free exploration, a controlled comparison, several implementations of one theme, or a coherent series. Before generation, explain in plain language:

- what remains fixed;
- what the images will visibly vary;
- how many images will be generated.

The batch is understandable only if a designer can see the intended differences without learning experiment terminology. A constraint such as “moderate roundness” sets the acceptable range; it must not collapse all directions into degree-only micro-variants. Compare distinct form topologies or surface relationships, and choose views and lighting that expose the changed relationship. Do not force a control-plus-variants structure when the design question calls for another arrangement.

## Revision

State what remains unchanged, identify the requested change’s main exterior trade-off, and offer only the minimum visibly different implementations needed. Do not reopen product strategy or the whole concept.

## Market Context

Apply India-market orientation through relevant industrial-design judgment: handheld comfort, honest volume, retail-distance recognition, close-view craft, likely case use, and suitable Cultural Expression. By default, devote roughly 80% of design attention to Functional Form and 20% to Cultural Expression. Translate one cultural source into form and CMF relationships; do not infer a single Indian taste, mix unrelated motifs, or paste decoration onto a resolved phone. Use a restrained culture-bearing atmosphere instead of a neutral-grey-only default background.

## Recommendation and waiver

When the user asks to use recommendations, proceed with the recommended exterior decisions. An explicit direct-generation request waives dialogue. Do not ask once per image in an accepted batch.

## Learning review

After showing every generated image, ask naturally: which images are liked or disliked, what should be preserved, and what is wrong. The user need not score images or explain Schema terminology. Translate the feedback into the smallest justified change to the current concept, this Dialogue, or the Design Judgment Schema.
