# 2026 Contemporary Baseline 0.1.0

This baseline is a versioned maturity floor, not one house style. It is frozen during each learning batch and changes only after evidence-backed review.

## Evidence window

Primary evidence comes from official 2025–2026 announced or shipping phones and manufacturer design stories. Earlier products expose legacy defaults. Concepts and adjacent industries may inform one Leading Departure but do not define production maturity.

## Maturity anchors

### Holistic silhouette

Treat silhouette, corner family, front/back transitions, frame profile, camera mass, and grip as one system. A family relationship is stronger when models share how they feel and transition, not merely a repeated decoration.

### Human thinness

Thinness is visual and tactile: controlled side profile, softened contact edges, disciplined camera transition, and believable space for controls and openings. Avoid both blunt slabs and impossible razor-thin bodies.

### Deliberate front

Use a high screen-to-body impression, controlled and visually even bezels, coherent display/body radii, and a quiet front-camera decision. The front is part of the product identity, not blank evidence attached to a decorated back.

### Integrated Camera Architecture

The camera can be quiet or dominant. In either case, its enclosure, individual hardware, rear surface, frame, shadow gaps, and balance must read as one authored relationship. Scale follows the thesis rather than a universal oversized-island formula.

### Material-led CMF

Colour and finish reveal form. Use a small number of physically legible zones, controlled gloss contrast, tactile-looking surfaces, and accents concentrated at meaningful boundaries or controls. Affordable positioning reduces process count and decorative noise, not design care.

### Family Genes

Carry two or three repeatable relationships across geometry, camera integration, controls, or CMF. Do not rely on a logo or one copied camera shape as the entire family identity.

### One Leading Departure

Advance one dominant relationship beyond the baseline. Keep the rest mature enough that the product could belong to a credible 2026 portfolio. Several unrelated novelty gestures weaken rather than strengthen contemporaneity.

## Legacy warning signs

Treat these as diagnostic warnings, not automatic shape bans:

- thick or visibly uneven bezels without a product reason;
- a generic rounded-rectangle camera patch carrying the whole identity;
- fake lens count, arbitrary metallic rings, and decorative apertures;
- glossy rainbow gradients or large graphic slogans used instead of material strategy;
- cyber lines, vents, and aggressive gaming motifs unrelated to parts or use;
- sharp slab edges, rear curvature, and camera protrusion designed as disconnected layers;
- premium signaled only by more gloss, gold, labels, or visual complexity;
- affordable signaled by imitation, filler detail, or weak junction quality.

A valid concept may use curves, flat sides, gradients, large cameras, or expressive graphics when the full relationship is coherent, current, and specific to the brief.
