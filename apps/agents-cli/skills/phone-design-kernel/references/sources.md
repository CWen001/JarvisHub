# 2026 Baseline Sources

Official manufacturer sources establish the initial evidence window. Marketing and engineering claims are not treated as independently validated facts; the Kernel admits only externally visible design relationships.

## Current mature form and family coherence

- [Samsung Galaxy S26 Series Design Story, 2026](https://design.samsung.com/global/contents/view/galaxy-s26) — supports silhouette as first impression, shared corner radii across a family, coherent front/back transitions, comfortable edge treatment, slimmer visual character, and iterative form–CMF development.
- [Apple iPhone Air, 2025](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/09/introducing-iphone-air-a-powerful-new-iphone-with-a-breakthrough-design/) — supports side profile as a distinct presentation proof, a rear plateau that integrates concentrated hardware, and thinness communicated through complete architecture rather than an isolated dimension. Durability, battery, and performance statements remain manufacturer claims.
- [Google Pixel 9 design details, 2024](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/google-pixel-9-series-design/) — supports “radical refinement,” sculpted corners, flat front/back surfaces, tactile finish hierarchy, a purposeful camera signature, portfolio cohesion, and designing with cases in mind. It is supporting context rather than the primary 2025–2026 window.

## Accessible and multi-price design evidence

- [Samsung Galaxy A56/A36 introduction, 2025](https://news.samsung.com/global/mwc-2025-powered-by-awesome-intelligence-introducing-the-galaxy-a56-5g-and-a36-5g) — supports slim flat frames, disciplined camera alignment, and controlled colour/finish differentiation in mainstream products.
- [TECNO CAMON 40 Pro 5G](https://www.tecno-mobile.com/phones/product-detail/product/camon-40-pro-5g/) — supports a side-axis camera composition, broad finish narratives, narrow-bezel display emphasis, and differentiated colourways in a globally distributed imaging product. Photography rankings and hardware performance remain external claims.
- [TECNO Spark 40 Pro+, 2025](https://www.tecno-mobile.com/phones/product-detail/product/spark-40-pro-+/) — supports thin-body visual positioning and curved-display identity in an accessible product. “World's first” and performance statements are not design evidence.
- [Infinix NOTE 50](https://www.infinixmobility.com/note-50) — retained as portfolio evidence; the official extract exposes insufficient design detail for an initial rule, so no specific form claim is admitted.
- [itel S25 Ultra](https://www.itel-life.com/ar/products/brand/press-room/view/301/) — supports the visible combination of a slim curved body, triple-camera decoration, and premium-coded display treatment at an affordable position. Durability and longevity statements remain manufacturer claims.

## India-oriented exterior evidence

- [Counterpoint Research, India smartphone market 2025, 2026](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/Premiumization-Gains-Strength-in-India-Smartphone-Market-in-2025-Apple-Records-its-Highest-Ever-Value-Share) — supports a growing premium segment, camera-led portfolio emphasis, offline financing, festival-led channel activity, distinctive design as a differentiator, and rising battery capacity. Market estimates do not establish a single national aesthetic.
- [IDC, India smartphone market 2025–2026](https://www.idc.com/resource-center/press-releases/india-smartphone-market-2025-2026/) — supports the coexistence of several price classes, premiumization, and strong offline-channel importance. Shipment and ASP estimates are product-context evidence, not exterior preference scores.
- [Counterpoint Research, India Smartphone Durability Consumer Study, 2025](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/india-smartphone-durability-consumer-study) — directionally supports durability, frame, water-resistance, and protective-accessory concerns. The study was commissioned by OPPO India and covers Tier 1/2 users, so it does not define nationwide preference.
- [TECNO CAMON 50 Ultra 5G India](https://www.tecno-mobile.in/products/mobile/camon-50-ultra-5g/) — supports the India-market coexistence of thin-body presentation, prominent three-camera hierarchy, expressive colourways, dedicated controls, and durability messaging. Performance statements remain manufacturer claims.
- [realme 14 Pro Series India design announcement](https://www.realme.com/in/newsroom/realme-14pro-series-5g-launching) — supports effect CMF, pearl texture, temperature-reactive colour, curved form, camera symbolism, and durability messaging in an India-market upper-tier product. It proves product presentation, not consumer preference.

## Presentation evidence

- [Samsung Galaxy S25 Design Story, 2025](https://design.samsung.com/global/contents/galaxy-s25/index.html) — uses a coordinated sequence: rear product grouping, front-corner crop, side view, colour grouping, and display crop.
- Apple iPhone Air presents rear colour views and side-profile views as separate coordinated images.
- [OPPO Find X9](https://www.oppo.com/en/smartphones/series-find-x/find-x9/) — supplements hero imagery with multiple product-viewer angles and a 360-degree viewer.

These sources support a Dual-View Hero as a useful first concept artifact, but not the claim that one hero can replace all later side and detail views. Cross-view consistency remains an empirical image-generation test.
