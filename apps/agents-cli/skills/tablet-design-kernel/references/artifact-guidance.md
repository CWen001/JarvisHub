# Tablet Artifact Guidance

This package preserves the current TypeScript mainline's nine registered professional visual targets. Concept Sketch is the first acceptance-backed executable path in JarvisHub. The remaining guidance may inform a requested native image turn, but does not create a target registry, prerequisite state machine, custom Tool, queue, or second Artifact authority.

All targets preserve the accepted design direction, use Jarvis-native references, and remain derived visual expressions rather than new product-design authorities.

## Concept Sketch

One low-information-density bare-tablet Hero establishing product identity. Show the Hero State, one primary perceptible outcome, and zero or one necessary subordinate clue. No board, sequence, dimensions, text-heavy callouts, case disguise, or equal-weight alternatives.

## Use Storyboard

An ordered sequence of real use moments showing posture, support, handling, state transitions, and product behavior. Preserve one product identity across every frame; do not invent new design facts to make the story work.

## Four Panel Promo

Exactly four visually ordered panels that communicate one concise product narrative. Maintain hierarchy and visual continuity rather than treating panels as four independent concepts.

## Six View Concept

Six complementary views of the same product identity for visual inspection. Keep proportions, nodes, CMF, states, and accessories consistent; do not use view count to introduce variants.

## Material / CMF Direction

A focused reading of accepted color, material, finish, reflection, texture, edge, contact-zone, node, and process-detail relationships. Do not choose a generic palette or detach swatches from product elements.

## Structure Concept

Explain visible structural layers, attachment, support, hinge/closure, and assembly intent without claiming hidden engineering validity or inventing internal mechanisms.

## Posture / Use Relationship

Show credible human-product posture, support, viewing, holding, input, and transition relationships. The image evaluates interaction geometry; it must not become portrait-led lifestyle advertising.

## Proportion / Dimension Direction

Explain relative scale, aspect, section, thickness, radius, footprint, and working geometry. Use qualitative or sourced measures only; never invent precision.

## Design Rationale Map

Connect design pressures to coordinated decisions and perceptible outcomes. Keep the product visually primary and use concise relationships rather than a dense requirements diagram.
