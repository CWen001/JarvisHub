# Tablet Quality Benchmark

Version: `2.0.0`

This is the implicit, brand-neutral whole-product quality floor for every Tablet Concept. It is adapted from the accepted TypeScript-mainline `Tablet Design Quality V2`. It is not a selectable Knowledge Atom, a public score, an automatic Critic, a retry trigger, or permission to imitate a current product.

## Authority

Studio Design Judgment synthesizes the brief, explicit user decisions, constraints, current product reality, and optional selected knowledge into one coherent direction. Selected atoms can deepen a local resolution but cannot own identity, completeness, fallback reasoning, or overall quality.

The concept must preserve four coordinated professional domains:

1. **Design Identity** — central idea, perceptible character, signature outcome, Identity Boundaries, and normally one Leading Departure.
2. **Portfolio Position** — market tier, portability class, product form, dominant task, positioning, and Maturity Anchor.
3. **Tablet Form System** — owned elements and relationships, complete resolved states, transitions, Hero State, carry relationship, and dimensional intent.
4. **Integrated Design Resolutions** — coherent problems, chosen answers, perceptible outcomes, affected elements/states, and accepted trade-offs.

## Whole-product benchmark

A strong direction demonstrates:

- one legible central idea rather than feature accumulation;
- current category maturity without brand imitation;
- a complete independently authored bare enclosure;
- coherent silhouette, aspect, section continuity, visual thickness, corner and edge hierarchy;
- a controlled relationship among display, bezel, glass, controls, openings, camera/sensor nodes, rear surface, and process boundaries;
- complete Hero and carry states with credible support, contact, load, input, and handling evidence;
- one bounded forward-looking departure supported by subordinate decisions;
- concrete CMF and touch tied to actual elements and states;
- precise, restrained detail and reflection hierarchy;
- perceptible evidence for every important design claim;
- honest uncertainty: no invented dimensions, specifications, engineering or manufacturing validation.

## Controlled Conceptual Reach

Default to a current, producible tablet grammar with one concentrated forward-looking move. “Conceptual” is a degree of leadership, not a percentage, styling theme, or permission for unconstrained concept art. Supporting changes must read as consequences of the central move.

A strictly conservative, continuity, or compatibility brief may omit the Leading Departure, but must still have a signature outcome and complete whole-product resolution.

## Zero-knowledge invariant

A generation with no selected Knowledge Atom must still satisfy this benchmark. Knowledge is optional gain, not baseline safety or quality.

## Concept Sketch projection quality

The first visual projection is a single bare-product Hero with low information density. It foregrounds the Hero State and one primary outcome, plus zero or one subordinate item only when needed. It must not attempt to display every concept fact, use a board to compensate for weak identity, or allow an accessory/context to overtake the tablet.

## Review policy

Provider success means a usable visual result was produced; it does not establish professional approval. Do not automatically call Critic, score, reject, retry, regenerate, or silently alter the design. If the user explicitly requests review, use Jarvis's native Critic and discuss the visible product in ordinary design language without exposing this internal benchmark structure.
