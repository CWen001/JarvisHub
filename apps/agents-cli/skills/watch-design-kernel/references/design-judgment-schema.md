# Watch Design Judgment Schema 0.6.0

This pre-generation model resolves a concrete Design State before Prompt authorship. It runs inside the existing design call, not as a rule list, judge, separate LLM call, persisted object, or Prompt suffix.

## Constraint dialect

Use the narrowest constraint that preserves professional judgment:

1. **Fixed value** for physical or artifact invariants only.
2. **Controlled choice** as `one-of [options] | equally precise alternative`. Record only the selected value and its visible consequence, never the menu.
3. **Cardinality** for composition budgets such as one primary carrier, two or three relationships, and one or two echoes.
4. **Relational quantity** for proportion and salience. Pair every soft term with its reference and visible consequence; `thin`, `quiet`, `low contrast`, or `Hero-visible` alone is unresolved.
5. **Derived default** from the accepted role and proven archetype. Defaults fill non-consequential gaps and never override the brief, accepted reference authority, or user decision.
6. **Relationship statement** for geometry and craft. State what each part does relative to its neighbour, substrate, boundary, and light rather than listing adjectives.

Controlled vocabularies are compact professional starting points, not closed taxonomies. An alternative must name the same kind of decision with equal precision.

## Compact Internal Design State

Resolve this template before writing the Prompt. Keep each line concrete and compact; do not expose the template to the user or create a separate runtime object.

```text
role: one selected product role
maturityPrior: provenArchetype; proportionEnvelope; thicknessBudget
designSpine: display/glass → upper case → main frame → lower case → attachment → strap/bracelet, with each boundary relationship stated
culturalGrammar: sourceRelationships[2..3]; abstractionOperation[1]
craftResolution.primaryCarrier: physicalPart; substrate; heroVisibleArea; thicknessShare
craftResolution.processBinding: process; visibleScaleAndDepth; edgeTermination; partBoundaryRule; lightResponse
craftResolution.composition: secondaryEchoes[1..2] {physicalPart; substrate; compatibleProcess; transformedRelationship}; quietZones
functionalResolution: primaryTimeOrState; physicalFunctionalScale; displayIndependence; secondaryInformation; interactionState; physicalControls
constructionResolution: materialZones {physicalPart; material; finish; textureScale; reflectivity; neighbourBoundary; lightResponse}; layerOrder; seamsAndTransitions; attachmentAndClosure
ornamentalResolution: optional subordinate physicalPart; boundedExtent
salienceOrder: firstRead; productDistanceRead; inspectionDistanceRead
heroProof: view; mustReveal; mustNotHide
```

For a series, add only:

```text
familyInheritance: parentSpine; sharedRelationships[2..3]; sharedCraftSyntax
variants[]: role; primaryVariationAxis[1]; craftResolution
```

## 1. Establish the maturity prior

Resolve the default chain in order:

```text
role → proven archetype → proportion envelope → thickness budget → carrier capacity
```

Use these controlled choices where applicable:

- role: `one-of [daily professional | sport-performance | outdoor-instrument | formal-craft | expressive-lifestyle] | equally precise alternative`;
- proven archetype: `one-of [softened rectangle | round | cushion/tonneau] | equally precise established watch topology`;
- visual thickness: `one-of [slim relative to archetype | balanced relative to archetype | robust relative to archetype]`, followed by the layer consequence;
- display-to-case relationship: `one-of [display-dominant | case-balanced | frame-emphasised]`, followed by the visible border consequence;
- curvature family: `one-of [taut | softened | fully rounded]`, propagated through glass, case, attachment, and controls.

Then resolve case aspect, control scale, case-to-strap ratio, attachment span, first strap drop, and visual mass distribution as relationships within that envelope. Set the thickness budget before adding carriers, inlays, guards, or layered metal; craft consumes the budget rather than stacking outside it.

When the brief leaves non-consequential choices open, default to the role-appropriate proven archetype, case-balanced proportions, an existing structural carrier, one translated secondary echo, a restrained supporting strap, and a complete-product Hero view. A theme, symbol, material metaphor, or desire for difference cannot choose the primary silhouette.

## 2. Construct the Design Spine

Resolve one continuous visual and assembly path:

```text
display/glass → upper case → main frame → lower case → attachment → strap/bracelet
```

For every boundary, select one primary relationship from `one-of [sits within | overlaps | reveals | grows from | terminates against] | equally precise geometric relationship`, then state its direction and visible consequence. The resulting chain owns the whole-product character.

## 3. Extract cultural grammar

Select two or three source relationships from `one-of [nested framing | broken continuity | interlocking rhythm | sparse field/dense border | positive/negative figure | radial order | line-weight change | layer sequence] | equally precise relationship`.

Select one abstraction operation from `one-of [compress | reveal | interlock | interrupt | repeat at another scale | continue across an assembly] | equally precise operation`. Record the transformed product relationship, not the option list.

A moon, lattice, water ripple, branch, cloud, or landscape is source material, not yet a product feature.

## 4. Resolve the primary Craft Carrier

Select exactly one real physical part from `one-of [upper rim | exposed carrier | sidewall plate | attachment shoulder | broad first bracelet link | clasp cover] | equally credible structural surface`. It must have enough visible area to carry the primary craft in a complete-product Hero frame. Record its substrate and share of the established thickness budget.

`Hero-visible` means the craft remains recognisable at complete-product distance without a macro crop. A narrow ledge cannot carry a complex scene; microscopic, hairline, or hidden treatment cannot carry primary identity. Prefer an existing structural surface over another decorative layer.

## 5. Bind process to substrate

Select one primary process from `one-of [engraving/etching | inlay/metal marquetry | bounded ceramic/enamel/lacquer-like insert | substrate-native weaving/moulding] | equally credible process`, then resolve its required relationship:

- engraving or etching: metal substrate, readable line width/depth, deliberate termination, directional highlight;
- inlay or metal marquetry: host recess, insert material, flush or proud condition, edge seam, thickness impression;
- ceramic/enamel/lacquer-like treatment: bounded cell or insert, depth and gloss relationship, protected termination;
- woven or moulded strap treatment: yarn colour or compound, weave density or moulded relief, rib, stitch, reinforcement, or perforation appropriate to a flexible substrate.

A flexible strap receives a substrate-native translation rather than a drawn metallic bar or rigid case pattern copied unchanged.

## 6. Compose craft loci

Compose the primary carrier with one or two secondary echoes and explicit quiet zones. Every echo uses one selected cultural relationship while changing scale or process for its substrate.

Default to one echo. Add the second only when it completes a cross-part relationship visible in the Hero frame. The composition remains identifiable without colour and does not reduce to an ordinary watch with a themed palette, screen wallpaper, or surface decal.

## 7. Resolve detail by purpose

Resolve in this order:

1. **Functional** — primary time or state, Physical Functional Scale, display independence, secondary information, interaction state, controls and openings. Put short non-semantic index marks only on a permanent non-display metal inner carrier, bezel, or rehaut using machining, engraving, etching, or inlay. Keep them physically outside the active screen so they remain when the electronic watch face changes.
2. **Construction** — each material zone's material, finish, texture scale, reflectivity, neighbour boundary and light response, plus layer order, seams, transitions, attachment and closure.
3. **Ornamental** — only optional subordinate treatment not already resolved by cultural grammar and Craft Resolution; record its physical part and bounded extent once.

Set salience in the same order. First read establishes product identity and primary function. Product distance reveals the Physical Functional Scale, controls, layer order, primary craft, and attachment. Inspection distance proves engraving, inlay, texture, seams, hardware, and closure.

Every soft salience term needs a reference and consequence. For example: `strap contrast lower than the case, noticed after the primary carrier`; `craft depth readable at product distance through a directional highlight`; `visually slim relative to the chosen archetype, with craft layers contained inside the upper-case envelope`.

The strap remains a quiet supporting part: at most one low-contrast, substrate-compatible echo on either its first segment or closure, not both.

## 8. Derive a series through inheritance

A series shares one parent Design Spine, two or three structural relationships, and one craft syntax:

```text
primary carrier → substrate-bound process → translated secondary echo → quiet zone
```

Each model changes exactly one role-driven primary axis and its Craft Resolution while preserving the parent relationships. Family identity comes from structure and craft composition rather than common colour or repeated motifs.

## 9. Compile the Hero proof

Choose one complete-product view that keeps the primary Craft Carrier recognisable while showing the full strap or bracelet and closure. Use grazing light to reveal relief, etching, inlay, layered-metal edges, or weave. The view proves the resolved design; it does not compensate for missing design with a macro panel or hidden crop.

## Readiness invariant

```text
role-derived maturity prior + thickness budget → Design Spine
→ selected cultural relationships + one abstraction operation
→ one Hero-visible structural carrier → substrate-bound process
→ one or two translated echoes + quiet zones
→ functional detail → construction detail → subordinate ornament → Hero proof
```

For a series:

```text
parent Design Spine → shared relationships → shared craft syntax → one role variation axis per model
```

Do not write the Prompt until every line in the Compact Internal Design State is either concretely resolved or explicitly non-applicable. After a learning batch, replace the earliest weak relationship before the next batch; do not append another prohibition or post-generation gate.
