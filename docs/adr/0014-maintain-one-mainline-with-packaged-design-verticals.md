# Maintain one mainline with packaged design verticals

The product will maintain one shared mainline shaped as a Shared Product Trunk with parallel vertical branches: unchanged Jarvis Core and Professional Workspace form the common execution layer, category-neutral Agent Workspace infrastructure forms the common product layer, and independently packaged Vertical Design Extensions such as Watch and Tablet supply only their professional branch. The development demo may expose several registered verticals so their portability can be exercised without long-lived product forks; customer-specific activation, authorization, tenancy, and deployment isolation are explicitly deferred until delivery requirements exist. Each vertical is a self-contained native Skill Package with its own professional references, manifest, validator, and Design Authority, while it must not own a Web brand descriptor, copy Product infrastructure, or introduce another Harness; one compile-time list of native Skill keys is the complete vertical registry. Tablet is migrated as a source-faithful portable professional package from the current TypeScript mainline: its Concept Schema, Knowledge Corpus, quality standard, and Artifact guidance are normalized into versioned Skill references, while its former Pure Pi runtime, frontend, provider, Session, and Gallery concerns are excluded and replaced by Jarvis-native execution. The resulting package is vendored, self-contained, and independently runnable inside JarvisHub: it records source repository, files, and revision in its manifest but has no build-time or runtime dependency on the sibling `tablet_pi` repository; future upstream changes require an explicit import, review, version bump, and validation. Tablet's first package may carry the complete professional corpus and downstream Artifact guidance, but its first executable and acceptance path is deliberately limited to Concept Sketch generation through Jarvis's native image path; the remaining Artifact paths will be added incrementally. Every Tablet generation must reuse Jarvis's existing `sourceEvidence` mechanism to record the applied BaseModel, knowledge catalog, selected Knowledge Atom, and applicable quality-benchmark revisions, matching Watch provenance without adding a Tablet database, Tool, or trace system. The Quality Benchmark constrains generation by default but does not imply an automatic Critic pass: review remains an explicit Jarvis-native request unless a later Tablet delivery stage defines a concrete quality gate. Tablet reuses Watch's proven Directional Design Dialogue protocol rather than its business content or Tablet Studio's old workflow: Jarvis asks only for a decisive missing fact, derives 3–6 Tablet-specific strategy cards from brief sufficiency, the Knowledge Corpus, Form System, Maturity Anchor, Leading Departure, CMF, and product states, and treats acceptance of one recommended composition as generation authorization; local revisions and derivative views do not repeat the dialogue. Implementation evolves from the proven Watch–Jarvis form: Tablet first mirrors its Skill, manifest, references, validation, native execution, and evidence boundaries; only infrastructure duplicated by these two real verticals is lifted into the Shared Product Trunk. The design must not prebuild a universal vertical framework, force both professional models into one super-schema, or add speculative Extension fields. This supersedes ADR-0002 and ADR-0011's exactly-one-extension installation and three-field Web Descriptor decisions while retaining the deep, deletable Extension Module principle.
