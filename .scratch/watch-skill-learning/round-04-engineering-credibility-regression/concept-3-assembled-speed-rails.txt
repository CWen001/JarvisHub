It must feel like credible modern outdoor equipment, not jewellery, a costume object, a generic camouflage watch, or a traditional analogue watch.

FIXED DESIGN LANGUAGE

This must remain the leanest and fastest concept, but every aggressive line must correspond to a separate retained part, seating boundary, or articulation.
