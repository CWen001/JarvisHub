DESIGN BRIEF
A contemporary Indian-jungle expedition smartwatch with a softened octagonal silhouette, broad protective shoulders, and controlled predator-like energy. It must feel unmistakably Indian through abstract stepped stone-carving geometry and precise traditional metalwork-like grooves—not through literal cultural symbols. It must feel like credible modern outdoor equipment, not jewellery, a costume object, a generic camouflage watch, or a traditional analogue watch.

FIXED DESIGN LANGUAGE
- Silhouette: substantial but not bloated softened octagon, broad shoulders, rounded transitions, large active display, compact protective perimeter, visually controlled layered thickness.
- Colour: deep Indian forest green, wet-rock charcoal black, a very small burnt-saffron-orange signal accent, and restrained aged-brass functional metal.
- Cultural translation: continuous stepped lines, nested geometric cuts, disciplined engraved channels, and hand-worked metal precision. Make this Indian character clearly visible at product-view distance while remaining abstract and manufacturable.
- Energy: masculine, fast, alert, and capable. Suggest a powerful jungle animal through tension, directional cuts, and asymmetrical rhythm only. No tiger head, animal graphic, realistic stripe print, paw mark, or ordinary camouflage.
- Materials: matte fibre-reinforced polymer or bead-blasted metal case, dark technical elastomer or rugged woven strap, satin aged-brass metal only at small functional boundaries. No glossy toy plastic and no gemstones.
